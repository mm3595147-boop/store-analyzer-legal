1. About the app

Store Analyzer is an embedded Shopify application that helps merchants identify issues in product information and inventory. This policy explains the information Store Analyzer accesses, how it is used, where it is processed, and how to contact us about privacy.

2. Information accessed and stored

With the Shopify read_products permission, Store Analyzer accesses product and variant information, including identifiers, titles, descriptions, images, prices, inventory-related fields, and product status.

To provide its service, the app stores the shop domain; Shopify authentication and session records, which may contain access credentials; audit summaries; and results for affected products, including product identifiers, titles, and issue categories. Technical access and error logs may also be processed for security, operations, and troubleshooting.

Store Analyzer does not request access to Shopify customer, order, or payment data, and is not designed to collect that information.

3. How information is used

We use this information to authenticate stores, perform product and inventory checks requested by merchants, display and retain audit findings, maintain service security and reliability, and provide support. Store Analyzer identifies issues; it does not automatically modify Shopify products or inventory.

4. Service providers and locations

The application and its primary operational database are hosted using Render in Frankfurt (EU Central), Germany. Encrypted off-site database backups are stored in a private Backblaze B2 bucket in the US East region, United States. These backups are encrypted before upload. Technical logs and operational information may be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers to operate the service.

Depending on your location, this means store-related information may be processed outside your country.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep store and audit information while it is needed to provide the app and fulfill applicable security, legal, and Shopify platform requirements. Stored audit summaries and affected-product results have different update lifecycles; newer completed audits can replace older affected-product detail records.

When an app is uninstalled or a valid Shopify privacy request is received, the app follows its applicable uninstall and privacy-webhook procedures. Deletion from encrypted backups and provider-operated logs or snapshots may not be immediate and is subject to applicable retention and lifecycle processes.

For information about your store's data or a deletion request, contact us using the email address below.

6. Security

Store Analyzer uses Shopify authentication, limits requested Shopify access to read_products, and applies shop-scoped data access controls. Backups are encrypted before being transferred to off-site storage. We limit access to operational and recovery credentials. No service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your privacy questions and requests

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if our practices, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies its current version.
